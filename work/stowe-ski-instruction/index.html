Instruction · Stowe Mountain Resort · 2013–2019

The explanation had to work on the next turn.

I spent six seasons teaching alpine skiing at Stowe Mountain Resort. It was technical communication with immediate feedback.

The work

Every skier brought a different body, confidence level, and way of processing an instruction. I learned to change the explanation without changing the goal, then watch the next turn to see whether it worked.

Why it belongs here

That habit still shapes my work. A clear explanation is useful only when the person receiving it can do something with it.
