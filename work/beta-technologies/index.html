The story

I produced Emma's Flight Paths story at BETA Technologies. She joined BETA through its partnership with the Vermont Adult Learning Center, bringing a real workforce pathway into a story about the people building electric aviation in Vermont.

The approach

The larger company was important, but Emma was the reason to watch. I built the piece around her own route into the work so the workforce program, the technology, and the organization arrived through a person instead of a list of claims.
