# Site copy review, 2026-09-23

This review covers the rendered copy on every public page after the EastRise and
Blue Cross removal. It applies `ames-writing:match-my-writing-style` for voice and
`ames-writing:humanizer` for AI patterns. Testimonial quotes and LinkedIn writing
cards were out of scope because they quote other people or published posts.

Oliver approved all 14 proposals on 2026-09-23, and they're live. The
practical technology intro gained "with a website or form" so it still makes
sense on the services index card, where it appears without its kicker. Each
proposal keeps the original claims. Items marked **added idea** contain
wording or framing that is mine rather than yours, so check those first.

## Already fixed (factual)

1. The homepage Ping Warden card still used the old positioning ("keeps local
   wireless discovery traffic from disrupting latency-sensitive work"). It now
   matches the product page: "Ping Warden stops the Wi‑Fi stutter that hits
   GeForce NOW and Xbox Cloud Gaming on a Mac by holding AWDL down while you
   play."
2. The practical technology page said "5 public software projects." The Work page
   lists six, so it now says 6.

## High priority

These are inaccurate, repeated, or confusing as they stand.

**3. Work index intro and filter framing repeat each other, and the role claim is
no longer true.** Both paragraphs end "Each card names the organization and my
role," but the role credits left with the EastRise cards.

- Current intro: "The projects include photography, video, communications,
  websites, and software. Each card names the organization and my role."
- Proposed intro: "It's photography, video, writing, websites, and software, and
  each card says who it was for."
- Current framing: "These include projects for BETA Technologies and other
  commissioned work. Each card names the organization and my role."
- Proposed framing: "Filter by organization, or scroll through all of it."
  (**added idea**)

**4. About page opening overstates two fields and ends on a sentence that could
describe anyone.** "Aerospace" rests on one documentary, and "sustainable
finance" and "organizational change" are vague stand-ins for the credit union.

- Current: "I've worked in aerospace, independent journalism, sustainable
  finance, science education, and organizational change. In each role, I
  translated complicated work for the public and helped run the systems that
  publish, measure, or support it."
- Proposed: "I've run a planetarium, raised money for a Vermont newsroom, led a
  credit union's social media through a merger, and made a documentary about
  electric aviation. Each job meant explaining something complicated to the
  public and running the systems that published and measured it."

**5. About page repeats its own list.** The second and third paragraphs each list
what you can do (plan, write, photograph, build; then direct, troubleshoot,
present, shoot).

- Current: "My work moves between strategy and production. I can plan a content
  program, write and edit the material, photograph the people involved, build the
  page or workflow, and check the data after publication. That range comes from a
  career spent inside small teams where the idea and execution rarely belong to
  separate people. I'm comfortable directing a shoot, troubleshooting a website,
  presenting a plan, or picking up the camera myself."
- Proposed: "I move between strategy and production, so I can plan a content
  program, write it, photograph the people in it, build the page, and check the
  numbers afterward. I've mostly worked on small teams, where the person with the
  idea usually has to make it happen too."

**6. Apple Core's intro reads as a limitation.** "It doesn't decide which
services or remote routes are available" is meant to say the owner decides.

- Current: "Apple Core lets an assistant use Calendar, Notes, Mail, Messages, and
  other services, but it doesn't decide which services or remote routes are
  available."
- Proposed: "Apple Core lets an assistant use Calendar, Notes, Mail, Messages, and
  more on my Mac, and I decide which services it can reach and whether any of
  them work remotely."

**7. Meta MCP's intro sounds like a flaw.** "It still exposes each platform's
permissions and failure modes" can read as a security problem.

- Current: "Meta MCP wraps seven Meta platforms in 200 tools, but it still
  exposes each platform's permissions and failure modes."
- Proposed: "Meta MCP puts seven Meta platforms behind 200 tools, and when a
  request fails, it tells you which permission or token caused it."

## Medium priority

These are voice and rhythm fixes: repeated openers, missing contractions, and
lines that could sit on anyone's site.

**8. Practical technology opens with "I work on" three times** (services index,
kicker, intro).

- Kicker, current: "I work on websites, forms, analytics, automations, and
  software."
- Kicker, proposed: "I fix the websites, forms, and workflows people have learned
  to work around."
- Intro, current: "I work on websites, forms, analytics, and small automations
  when the problem sits behind what the public sees. I'm usually looking for a
  repeated manual step or broken path that everyone has learned to tolerate."
- Intro, proposed: "Most of the time the problem sits behind what the public
  sees. I look for the manual step everyone repeats or the broken path everyone
  has learned to tolerate, and then I fix that instead of rebuilding everything."
  (**added idea**: the "instead of rebuilding" clause comes from the page's own
  FAQ answer.)

**9. Strategy and content restates itself.** The kicker says "I plan, write,
publish, and measure content," and the next line starts "I plan and write
content."

- Kicker, proposed: "I find out what your readers are asking, then write the
  answers and check whether they worked."
- Proof paragraph, current: "At VSECU, now EastRise, this approach supported 53
  published financial education articles. It also helped grow annual Facebook
  impressions by 319%."
- Proof paragraph, proposed: "At VSECU, which is now EastRise, I wrote 53
  financial education articles this way, and the same planning helped grow
  annual Facebook impressions by 319%."

**10. The software heading differs between pages.** The homepage says "I build
software for problems I keep running into." The Work page says "Software for
problems I kept running into." I'd use the homepage version on both, since it's
actor-first and present tense.

**11. The Green Mountain Community Fitness hub opens with filler.**

- Current: "This page collects two photography assignments for Green Mountain
  Community Fitness."
- Proposed: "I've done two photography assignments for Green Mountain Community
  Fitness: a Valentine's Day competition and a one-to-one bike fitting."

**12. Drone photography closes on an abstraction.**

- Current: "The collection spans several years and places and returns to lines,
  shapes, and changes in scale."
- Proposed: "I made these over about three years, and I kept coming back to lines,
  patterns, and how small everything looks from up there." (**added idea**: "how
  small everything looks" reinterprets "changes in scale.")

**13. The 404 page has no contraction and no way forward.**

- Current: "That page does not exist or has moved."
- Proposed: "That page doesn't exist or has moved. Try the work page or send me a
  note." (link both)
