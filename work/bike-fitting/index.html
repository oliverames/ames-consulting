Documentary photography · Green Mountain Community Fitness · 2025

A bike fitting is a story told in small adjustments.

This shoot followed the practical, one-to-one work of fitting a rider to a bike at Green Mountain Community Fitness.

The assignment

Show the service clearly enough that a prospective rider can understand what happens, while keeping the photographs grounded in the relationship between the fitter, the rider, and the machine.

The approach

I photographed the full process and the details that make the expertise visible: posture, measurement, observation, tools, contact points, and repeated adjustments. The series works as a sequence, but each frame can also stand alone in a service page or social post.
