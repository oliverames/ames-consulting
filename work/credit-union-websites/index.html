Digital platforms · VSECU and EastRise · 2021–2024

Two credit union websites, rebuilt around clearer paths.

I was integral to the credit-union team for the PixelSpoke redesigns of vsecu.com and eastrise.com.

My role

My work included content migration, extensive quality assurance, image curation, photography, and coding support.

The VSECU redesign received a 2022 Silver Davey Award. The EastRise site launched with the new brand in September 2024.

A visual library rooted in Vermont

The redesigned sites used photographs of real members, employees, and community events in Vermont settings. That image library made the digital experience specific to the institution rather than interchangeable with any bank.
