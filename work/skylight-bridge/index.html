Skylight Bridge

macOS app

The problem

Family information often lives in several good systems that do not talk to one another. Skylight Bridge connects deliberately selected albums, reminder lists, recipes, and chores without asking a family to abandon the Apple apps they already use.

The product

Photos move one way to Skylight. Reminders, recipes, and chores can sync in both directions. Every connection is opt-in, and an activity log makes recent changes visible instead of leaving the sync process mysterious.

The boundary

The app uses Skylight's private API, so it is presented as an independent, source-available project rather than an official integration. The macOS app is signed and notarized, and an iOS companion is in development.
