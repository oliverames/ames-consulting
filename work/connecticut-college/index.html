Digital storytelling · 2013–2015

Where digital storytelling became the job.

At Connecticut College, I developed content for college blogs and social channels, supported donor events, and contributed to a major website redesign.

The work

The assignment moved between writing, publishing, event support, and the practical details of a large institutional website. It was the point when something I had been doing instinctively became a profession.

What stayed with me

The audience was never one group. Students, alumni, donors, and prospective families each arrived with different questions, so the work had to be clear about who it was helping and why.
