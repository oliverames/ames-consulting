Documentary photography · Vermont Foodbank · January 21, 2026

Vermont Foodbank Volunteer Day

Volunteers packed food for distribution inside the Vermont Foodbank warehouse. I photographed the work itself, the people moving through it, and the small details that made the day feel specific.

38 photographs

Work with a rhythm of its own

The warehouse already had a visual system: pallets, rollers, open boxes, stacked cans, and people finding a pace together. I moved between the full process and the moments that showed concentration, cooperation, and the occasional laugh.

The final set gives the Vermont Foodbank a complete record of the volunteer day, with individual photographs that can also work across future stories, volunteer recruitment, and social posts.
